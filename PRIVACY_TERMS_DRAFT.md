# Privacy policy — starter draft

Community Finder is designed to help people discover local resources and activities.

## Location
The app may request device location to find nearby listings. Location should be used only for the requested discovery feature unless the user separately consents to storage or another use.

## Information users provide
Depending on features enabled, the service may receive saved listings, reports, account information, or organization-submitted information.

## Public resource information
Listings may contain information obtained from public sources or submitted by organizations. Each listing should retain a source and verification timestamp where available.

## Data retention and deletion
Provide users with a way to request deletion of account data. Retain only information necessary for security, legal obligations, or the operation of requested features.

## Security
Use encryption in transit, least-privilege database access, authenticated admin tools, and regular review of access permissions.

## Disclaimer
Resource information can change. Users should confirm important details such as eligibility, availability, hours, cost, and contact information with the provider.

This is a starter draft, not legal advice. Have counsel review it before publication.

# Terms — starter draft

The service helps users discover community resources and activities. Listings may be supplied by public sources, organizations, or community members. The service does not guarantee availability, eligibility, quality, or completeness of a listing. Users are responsible for confirming details with providers. Organizations are responsible for the accuracy of information they submit.
